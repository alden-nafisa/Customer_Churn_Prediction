### ✅ **2. Revenue at Risk Calculation** 
**Formula Lengkap dengan 5 Komponen**:

```
revenue_at_risk = Base MRR × Contract Factor × Payment Health × Engagement Health × Satisfaction Health
```

**Komponen Detail**:

| Komponen | Perhitungan | Contoh |
|----------|------------|--------|
| **Base MRR** | payment_value (Monthly) atau payment_value/12 (Annual) | $112.58 atau $89.52 |
| **Contract Factor** | Annual=0.7, Monthly=1.0 | 1.0 untuk Starter |
| **Payment Health** | on_time(0.5) + dunning(0.3) + delay(0.2) | 0.965 (hampir sempurna) |
| **Engagement Health** | activity(0.4) + adoption(0.35) + consistency(0.25) | 0.836 (engaged) |
| **Satisfaction Health** | nps(0.5) + support(0.25) + resolution(0.25) | 0.473 (at risk) |

**Contoh Kalkulasi Customer C-0001**:
- Base MRR: $112.58
- Contract Factor: 1.0 (Monthly)
- Payment Health: 0.965
- Engagement Health: 0.836
- Satisfaction Health: 0.473
- **Revenue at Risk = $42.88/bulan = $514.56/tahun** ❌

### ✅ **3. Preprocessing Strategy (Beyond SMOTE)**

**Alternatif SMOTE**:
- ✅ **Stratified Train-Test Split** (70-30) → Maintain churn ratio per plan
- ✅ **Class Weight Balancing** → Automatic weights di XGBoost/CATBoost
- ✅ **Threshold Tuning** → Optimize prediction boundary
- ✅ **Outlier Handling** → IQR capping per plan type
- ✅ **Low Variance Removal** → Drop features with var < 0.01
- ✅ **Correlation Filtering** → Remove corr > 0.95

**Keuntungan**: Preserves natural distribution tanpa artificial resampling

